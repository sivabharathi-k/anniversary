HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================

Place your 6 photos in this folder with these exact filenames:

  photo1.jpg  →  upper left polaroid
  photo2.jpg  →  upper right polaroid
  photo3.jpg  →  CENTER (main) polaroid
  photo4.jpg  →  lower left polaroid
  photo5.jpg  →  lower right polaroid
  photo6.jpg  →  mid right polaroid

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp

The website will automatically display them when you open index.html.

TO CHANGE CAPTIONS:
  Open js/memories.js and edit the `caption` fields in the `memories` array at the top of the file.

TO UPLOAD PHOTOS DIRECTLY IN THE BROWSER (preview mode):
  Click any polaroid frame while the page is open. 
  A file picker will appear. Choose any image from your device.
  The photo will appear immediately inside that frame.
  (This is a preview only — it does not save to disk.)
